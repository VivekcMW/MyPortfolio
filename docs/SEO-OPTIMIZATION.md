- Service offerings (UX Design, Product Management, AI Strategy, Design Systems)
- Geographic service areas (India, US, Singapore, Global)
- Provider information

#### **FAQPage Schema**
- 5 common questions with detailed answers:
  1. What does Vivekanand Choudhari do?
  2. What is his expertise?
  3. Where is he located?
  4. What industries has he worked in?
  5. How to contact him?
